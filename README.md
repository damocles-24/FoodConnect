# 🍽️ FoodConnect

**FoodConnect** is a Multi-Restaurant Online Ordering Platform developed as a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) Capstone Project.

The system allows customers to browse restaurants, place orders, and track deliveries while providing restaurant owners with complete management tools through dedicated dashboards.

---

# 🚀 Features

## Customer
- Restaurant browsing
- Food ordering
- Shopping cart
- Checkout
- Order tracking
- Email verification

## Restaurant Owner
- Dashboard
- Restaurant Management
- Product Management
- Order Management
- Sales Reports
- Notifications
- Staff Management
- Restaurant Settings

## Cashier
- Incoming Orders
- Order Processing
- QR Verification (Planned)
- Receipt Printing

## Delivery Staff
- Assigned Deliveries
- Delivery Status Updates

## Admin
- Restaurant Approval
- Platform Management
- Delivery Coordinator (Planned)
