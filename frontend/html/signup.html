1. Account Registration

Customers must provide accurate and complete information when creating and maintaining a FoodConnect account. Customers are responsible for protecting their account credentials.

2. Ordering and Transactions

Submitted orders represent a request to purchase products from participating restaurants. Order changes, cancellations, and refunds follow applicable FoodConnect and restaurant policies.

3. Payments

Customers authorize FoodConnect and its payment partners to process valid charges related to their transactions.

4. Restaurant Partners

FoodConnect connects customers with independent restaurant partners. Product preparation, availability, and food quality remain the responsibility of each restaurant.

5. User Conduct

Users must not misuse the platform, submit fraudulent orders, or attempt unauthorized access.

6. Privacy and Data Protection

FoodConnect collects and processes information necessary to provide ordering, account management, communication, and service improvements.
